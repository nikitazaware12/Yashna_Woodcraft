Our story

A workshop in Pune, a promise to every client

Yashna Woodcraft is a furniture manufacturer based in Baner, Pune. We make sofas, tables, beds, wall units and custom pieces for families, offices, hotels and restaurants.

Because we make everything ourselves, you talk directly to the people building your furniture. That means clear answers, fair pricing and pieces that fit your space exactly.
