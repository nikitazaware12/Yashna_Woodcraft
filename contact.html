FAQ

Questions we are often asked

Can I order a custom size or design?

Yes. Most of our work is made to measure. Share your sizes, photos or a sketch and we will suggest design, wood and finish.

How do I get a quote?

Message us on WhatsApp or send an email with what you need. We reply with next steps and a clear quote once the design is clear.

Do you deliver and install?

Yes. We deliver carefully and our team installs the furniture on site.

Which materials do you use?

Solid wood, engineered boards, veneers, fabrics and leather. We help you choose what suits the piece and your budget.

Can I visit the workshop?

You are welcome. Please call or message us first so we can confirm a time. We are in Baner, Pune.
