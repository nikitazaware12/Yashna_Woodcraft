Care guide

Keep it looking new

Simple habits that make furniture last for years.

  • 1Dust gentlyWipe with a soft dry cloth. Avoid rough or very wet cloths.
  • 2Avoid direct sunLong hours of strong sunlight can fade wood and fabric. Use curtains or move pieces.
  • 3Wipe spills fastBlot liquids straight away and dry the surface. Use coasters and mats.
  • 4Keep away from heatKeep furniture away from heaters, radiators and very damp walls.
  • 5Polish occasionallyUse a suitable furniture polish a few times a year to keep the finish fresh.
  • 6Lift, do not dragLift heavy pieces when moving them, and use felt pads under legs.
