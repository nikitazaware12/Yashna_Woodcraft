Terms & privacy

Plain-language notes on how enquiries, products and orders work.

Enquiries

When you send an enquiry through this website, the message opens in WhatsApp or your email app. Nothing is stored on this website. What you send is shared only with Yashna Woodcraft.

How we use your details

We use your name, phone number, email and requirements only to reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote.

Products & prices

Products shown are samples of our range. Prices depend on size, wood, finish and quantity, and are confirmed in a written quote. Colours may differ slightly from photos.

Custom orders

Custom pieces are made after the design, material and price are agreed. Timelines are confirmed with the quote.
